## 🔥 Key Features

- **⚡ Metabolic Calculation Engine:** Instant BMR & TDEE calculation based on age, gender, height, weight, and activity level.
- **🥗 Custom 6-Meal Diet Blueprint:** Auto-generated macro breakdowns (Protein, Carbs, Fats, Water) with customizable food preferences.
- **🔄 Dynamic Food Switcher:** Seamlessly toggle between **100% Pure Veg**, **Eggetarian**, and **Non-Veg** meal plans.
- **📊 Profile & Body Trackers:** Track weight history trends, body measurements (Waist, Hips, Chest, Visceral Fat), and progress photo comparisons.
- **🖨️ One-Click PDF Export:** Download or print a clean, print-friendly copy of the custom diet blueprint.
- **💬 Direct Coaching Integration:** Quick WhatsApp integration for 1-on-1 personalized metabolic coaching programs.
